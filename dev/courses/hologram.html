Hologram Technology Course

Hologram technology focuses on creating three-dimensional visual projections that appear in physical space without the need for special glasses. This course explores holographic imaging, projection systems, and real-world applications in education, healthcare, entertainment, and defense.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Holography
  • 3D Projection Systems
  • Holographic Display Technologies
  • Medical and Industrial Applications
  • Future Holographic Communication
