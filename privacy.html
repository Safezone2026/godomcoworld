Privacy Policy

Godomcoworld is an educational and digital marketplace application built within the Pi Network ecosystem.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Godomcoworld may process information such as your Pi username, account identifier, wallet address, support messages, and transaction records. Pi Network provides authentication information when you sign in through Pi Browser. We process information needed to operate, secure, and support the services.

Pi Network Data

When you authenticate with Pi Network, we may receive information such as your Pi user identifier and username. Certain information, including account identifiers, wallet addresses, and transaction records, may be stored by our systems where necessary to provide app features, maintain transaction records, and support users. We do not sell personal information.

Payments

Payments within Godomcoworld are processed using Pi Network’s payment system. Godomcoworld does not have access to users’ wallet private keys or sensitive financial information.

Use of Information

Information received through Pi Network is used to authenticate users, support Pi payments, provide app features, maintain transaction records, and improve the service.

Data Sharing

Godomcoworld does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by Pi Network and trusted service providers where necessary to authenticate users, operate the application, process Pi transactions, provide hosting, maintain security, and deliver customer support. Such processing is limited to what is reasonably necessary to provide and protect the services.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app evolves. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

Godomcoworld operates within the Pi Network ecosystem and follows Pi Network platform guidelines.
