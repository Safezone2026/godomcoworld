1st Industrial Revolution

Timeline: 1760 – 1840

Major Innovations

  • Steam engine
  • Mechanized textile machines
  • Railway transportation
  • Iron production

Key Innovators

  • James Watt – Improved steam engine
  • Richard Arkwright – Water frame textile machine
  • George Stephenson – Steam locomotives

This revolution began in Britain and transformed economies from agriculture to industrial manufacturing.
